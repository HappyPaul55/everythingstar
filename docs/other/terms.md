EverythingStar is a free browser rhythm game, published by **HappyPaul55**. These
terms say what you may do with the site, what the licence covers, and what the
game does not promise. They are short because there is little to agree to: there
is no account, nothing to buy, and no server of this project's own.

## The game is free

You can play EverythingStar in a browser, install it as a web app, and download
songs to play offline. None of it costs anything, and none of it needs an
account. The site is a set of static files served by Cloudflare; it sells
nothing and trades in nothing.

## What you may do

- **Play it**, alone or with other people, at home or in public.
- **Link to it** — the game page at
  [everythingstar.happypaul55.com/play](https://everythingstar.happypaul55.com/play),
  the link entries that add a playlist or a song, or any page of the
  [documentation](https://everythingstar.happypaul55.com/docs).
- **Install it and save songs for offline play.** The copies on your device are
  yours to keep or delete.
- **Share a run's brag card**, which the results screen makes and downloads as a
  file.
- **Read, change, run and self-host the code** under its licence, including your own
  copy of the site.

A recording of a run also contains somebody else's song, and a screenshot usually
contains somebody else's artwork. That part is not ours to license — see
[Songs and audio are not ours](#songs-and-audio-are-not-ours).

## What you may not do

- **Use the game to distribute audio you have no right to distribute.** A

  playlist names, and it cannot authorise a copy of a song.
- **Attack or overload the site or its host**, or try to get around the host's
  protections.
- **Misrepresent who made it** — do not present a modified copy as the original,
  or claim an affiliation with HappyPaul55 that you do not have.
- **Remove the licence and attribution notices** from a copy of the code, or from
  a copy of the game's sounds, fonts or artwork.

## Songs and audio are not ours

The game ships no songs. Everything it plays is fetched from a playlist at a URL:
the built-in library is the separate
[EverythingStar FOSS](https://github.com/HappyPaul55/everythingstar-foss)
repository, and any other playlist belongs to the service that publishes it. The
songs, lyrics, charts, recordings and cover art are under the licences those
publishers state, not these terms.

[Copyright and takedown](copyright.md) explains where each part actually lives and

a playlist, you are responsible for having the right to use it and for what your
copy fetches.

## No warranty

The game is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind, express or implied,
including any warranty that it suits a particular purpose. It is a small project
with one maintainer: it may change, break or be unavailable, a song's host may
take a song away, and a feature may be dropped.

Nothing is guaranteed to be kept. Your settings, band, scores, library and
downloaded songs live in your browser and go when that storage is cleared or
evicted; [Privacy](privacy.md) says what is stored and where. Keep your own copy of
anything you would miss — the source is on GitHub, and a run's brag card can be
downloaded.

## Your data and accounts

There are no accounts, and the game does not ask who you are. What it keeps stays
in your browser, and [Privacy](privacy.md) is the full account of it. There is
nothing to cancel and nothing to pay.
