The **Jukebox** is EverythingStar's listening mode. It is in the same game as Quick Play and the band's Karaoke — the same library, the same volume, the same screen — but there is no band, no notes to hit and no score. It picks a song, plays it, shows its words and moves on.

[The Jukebox: the song's cover as the spinning record, the timed words beside it, and the queue's next song underneath.](shot:jukebox-mode)

## Opening it

**Jukebox** is the smaller door under **Quick Play**, **Options** and **Credits** on the main menu. It starts with whatever the shuffle finds and keeps playing until you leave. Escape, or a gamepad's B, goes back to the menu.

## The record and the words

The record is the song's **cover art** in the middle of a vinyl disc. It turns while the song is playing and stops when it is paused. If the song has no cover, its drawn tile takes the label's place.

The words are the song's own timed lyrics, not a transcription made later. The live line is bright and the word being heard is picked out; the lines around it fade so the reading column is not a wall of text. On a wide screen the words sit beside the record. On a phone, or a portrait window, the record is on top and the words are underneath.

[The picker: search the library, then Play now, Play next or Add to queue.](shot:jukebox-picker)

## Playing and choosing

The transport has **play/pause**, **next**, and a progress bar with the time. The shuffle takes every song the library can reach — playlists, downloads, folders and `.sng` packages. A song whose sound is a YouTube video plays the video's audio; the record stays the picture.

**Pick a song** opens the library in the same searchable, A–Z list Quick Play uses. Choose a row, then choose what to do with it:

- **Play now** interrupts the current song. The current song keeps playing while the new one's document and words are loaded, then the switch is made as soon as its sound can play. A song that will not play leaves the current one playing and says which one failed.
- **Play next** puts the song at the front of your queue.
- **Add to queue** puts it at the end. Adding the same song twice does not queue it twice.

The queue is only the songs **you** chose. The shuffle is the filler behind it: when the queue is empty the Jukebox plays the shuffle, and those songs never appear in the queue count. **Queue** in the header opens the list, where a song can be removed or the whole queue cleared.

Leaving the Jukebox pauses it and remembers where the song was. Coming back resumes the same song from that point with the queue intact.

## When there are no words

An instrumental has no timed lyrics for the game to read, so the record takes the screen and the words column says so. A song whose lyrics chart could not be read says that too; it keeps playing.

## Jukebox, Listen and Quick Play

- **Quick Play** is where you choose a song and a band to play it.
- **Listen** is an unscored run of one song on the stage, with the words on the melody region. It is still a run: the stage, the entrance and the song's own clock.
- **Jukebox** is not a run. It is the room's record player: no band, no judging, no results and nothing on the board.

A library with nothing in it sends you to Quick Play to add a playlist. A song that cannot be played is named and skipped; if nothing in the library will play, the Jukebox says so rather than sitting in silence.
