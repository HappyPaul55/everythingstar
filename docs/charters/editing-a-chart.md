The charter is a chart editor built into EverythingStar. It is a tool, not a game
mode: open it from **Charter mode** on the main menu, or go straight to
[`/charter`](/charter).

## Open a song

Pick a song from your library, open a folder from this machine, or add a playlist
URL. The charter reads **every** instrument chart the song carries, not only the one
a band would play.

## Edit

- A note is a **strum** or a **hopo**. Tapping is how a controller plays it — a key,
  a dance pad or a phone — not a third kind of note.
- **Click** an empty cell to add a note at that column and moment.
- **Drag** across empty cells to paint notes.
- **Drag a note** to move it; the other selected notes move with it.
- **Click** a note to select it; **Shift-click** adds to the selection. The footer
  shows its exact time, column, length and kind.
- **Double-click** a highway to select every note in that chart.
- Use **Snap** to choose the grid, and **Quantize** to pull the selection onto it.
- **Copy / Paste** moves a selection to the playhead, including into another chart.
- **Undo / Redo** covers every edit and a whole take as one step.

`Space` plays and pauses. Arrow keys nudge the selection by a column or a grid step;
hold Shift and an up/down arrow to nudge by a bar. Delete removes the selection.

## Show more than one chart

A song can have any number of charts. Up to **eight** panes are visible at once — for
example the four guitar levels beside the four bass levels. Open **Charts** to show or
hide a chart, choose the one you are editing, or make a new chart at any instrument
and level.

## Record a take

Choose the chart you are editing, press **Record**, and play with the keyboard, a
gamepad or a MIDI input. A strum takes the frets held at that moment; a tapped
instrument turns each press into an ordinary strum note; a held fret is a sustain.
Tapping is the controller's way of playing the note, not a third note kind. The take starts at the
playhead and stops when you press Record again (or Escape). **Replace section** is the
default; **Overdub** lays the take over what is already there. **Quantize** snaps the
take as it is captured.

## Download

**Download .chart** writes a Clone Hero / YARG `.chart` containing every instrument
chart the format can carry. To play it in EverythingStar, put it beside the song's
audio as `song.ogg` in a folder and open that folder from **Playlists**. To edit it in
Clone Hero or YARG, use the same folder.

The charter does not upload anything and does not write back to a playlist's file.
Your work lives in the tab until you download it.
