# Code of conduct

EverythingStar is a small project with one maintainer and a lot of opinions about
rhythm games. The short version: **be decent, argue about the code, assume good
faith, and do not make the room worse for anyone.**

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any other
space where somebody is taking part in this project.

## What that means in practice

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This reads every frame and should be
  memoised" is useful; "you clearly do not understand React" is not.
- **Assume the other person is trying.** Most disagreements here are about a word's
  meaning (see the vocabulary in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)) or a trade-off somebody
  has already thought about.
- **Be patient with questions.** A chart format is obscure until it is not, and
  "read the docs" is a worse answer than a link and a sentence.
- **No harassment, discrimination or sexual attention.** That includes slurs,
  threats, sustained disruption, and publishing somebody's private information.
- **No spam.** Advertising a service, a playlist or a product in an issue or a pull
  request is not a contribution.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through **<https://www.happypaul55.com>**, and say
it is about EverythingStar's code of conduct. Reports are read privately and are not
shared without your agreement.

The maintainer may, at their discretion: ask for a change of tone, edit or remove a
comment, lock a thread, close a contribution, or block an account from the project.
A decision can be appealed once, in writing, and will be looked at again by the same
person — there is no committee here, and pretending otherwise would be worse.

This is a hobby project. Nobody is entitled to have a contribution merged, and the
maintainer is allowed to say no to a change that is unwelcome even if it is correct.
